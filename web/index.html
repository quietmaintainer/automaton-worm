Cell rules
  1. Open cells spread food scent. The scent fades with distance and after a pellet is eaten.
  2. Worm ends request space tickets. An open cell grants at most one ticket per round.
  3. When both ends are blocked, a worm can ask a neighbor to move. Wave mode lets a blocked recipient forward the request.
  4. Every grid cell runs the same rule, including cells with pellets and cells in the background.
